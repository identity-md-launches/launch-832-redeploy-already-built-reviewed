A GAME WITH NO OWNER

Learn the rules.

01

Trade to fill the pool.

Every buy and sell pays an ETH hook fee. It starts at 25% and falls linearly to 2.5% over 30 minutes. Of that hook fee, 92% goes to the throne pool and 8% to the team's pull-based credit. The factory's 1.25% pool fee is additional.

02

Buy your way to the throne.

After anti-snipe ends, one buy at or above the current throne price takes the throne. Small buys never add up. The new price is 1.2× the ETH paid, including the hook fee, and decays continuously to half each hour, down to 0.01 ETH. An empty throne costs 0.01 ETH.

03

Hold to earn.

Income accrues each second, with the throne pool retaining approximately 98% per hour without new fees. Income from a reign vests after five minutes; any earlier end forfeits it. Vested booked credits remain claimable after losing the throne. This is variable game income funded by trades.

04

Leave, and lose the crown.

Selling through the official router ends the reign immediately. A holding shortfall can be detected by any swap, claim, or permissionless dethrone call; provisional and unverified income is forfeited. Transfers have no callback. Exits and restorations between checks, and foreign-router sales that leave enough balance, cannot be detected.

Fixed from the first block.

1,000,000,000 KING. 18 decimals. Minted once to the launch factory. No token transfer fees, minting powers, limits, pausing, upgrades, or owner. Hook callbacks act on initialization and swaps, never transfers or liquidity changes. Only the official KingRouter can authenticate a buyer for a takeover; this app always uses it.

Launch terms: 900,000,000 KING (90%) in single-sided liquidity, 0% to the requester, starting total-supply market cap 10 ETH. The remaining 10% follows the launch network's contributor policy. Market cap is an initial price setting, not a reserve or redemption promise.

Transaction ordering, bots, and sequencer delays can affect a takeover. “Must take” protects the execution of your buy; it does not guarantee how long your reign lasts.
